---
title: "The Rose That Would Not Wilt"
lang: en
category: poetry
date: 2026-11-04
tags: [roses, love]
translationKey: wilting-rose
---
You left a rose on my windowsill
and a silence the shape of your hand.
I water both.

The petals darken but do not fall.
Some devotions refuse to end
and call it keeping faith.
